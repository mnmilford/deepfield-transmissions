## Deeper Dive
The transition from 'command and control' to 'intent-based integration' is the defining shift of 2026. In the labs I've been tracking, they aren't just looking at how a human can move a cursor with their mind; they're looking at how a distributed agentic swarm can anticipate a user's cognitive load and offload complex decision-making before the user even feels the fatigue.

In Singapore, the focus is intensely practical. While Western debates center on the philosophy of consciousness, the teams here are building the low-latency hardware necessary for a 'Human Tesla OS'—a seamless background layer that manages everything from your financial health to your physiological homeostasis. The DNI isn't a peripheral; it's the new bus for the human experience.
