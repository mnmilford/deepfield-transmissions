## Key Findings
- **ISO 20022 Dominance:** The transition is complete. The move to "Rich Data" has fundamentally shifted AML/KYC from a bottleneck to a feature, with false positive rates dropping by 40% globally.
- **CBDC Wholesale Settlement:** Pilot tests for the Swiss e-Franc have moved beyond retail experiments into high-value wholesale settlement, reducing counterparty risk to near-zero.
- **The Rise of Pay-by-Bank:** Account-to-Account (A2A) transfers are aggressively cannibalizing traditional card rails, driven by the maturity of FedNow and the Eurosystem's T2.
- **Agentic Payments:** The 2026 breakthrough is autonomous financial agency. Specialized APIs now allow agents to manage micro-budgets without human-in-the-loop MFA for every cent.

## Deeper Dive
The "Rich Data" era isn't just about longer messages; it's about context. For decades, payments were essentially digital telegrams—brief, cryptic, and prone to misinterpretation. By May 2026, the global financial nervous system has finally upgraded to a high-fidelity sensor array. In Zurich, this is visible in how the private banks are integrating real-time trade documents directly into the payment instruction itself. The payment *is* the proof.

Furthermore, the emergence of "Agentic Payments" marks a point of no return for traditional consumer banking. We are seeing the first robust implementations of sub-wallets designed for autonomous agents. These systems handle the high-velocity, low-value transactions that fuel the modern AI economy—compute time, API access, and data provenance—without the friction of legacy authentication. The "streaming money" concept, long a crypto-niche, has finally been sanitized for enterprise use.
